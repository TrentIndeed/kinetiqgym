Engineering

Building something difficult?

Kinetiq Gym combines mechanical design, CNC machining, additive manufacturing, embedded electronics, motor control, real-time software, and rapid prototyping. I take on a limited number of custom engineering projects where that same stack is useful.

Where it fits

  • Motorized fitness or rehab equipment
  • Robotics and actuator prototypes
  • Cable-driven mechanisms
  • Custom motor-control systems
  • Prototype hardware that needs CNC, 3D-printed parts and electronics working together

What you get

A working answer to the engineering problem: the mechanism, the motor and its control, the electronics and the software designed together, and prototype hardware made in-house so a design can be tested and changed in days rather than weeks.

Kinetiq Gym is the public example. Its build preview and build status show how the work is done.
