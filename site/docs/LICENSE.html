What you may do

Build the machine, use it, study it, modify it and share your changes. If you distribute a modified design or software, publish your modified source under the same licence and keep the notices.

The name and logo are not licensed

KINETIQ, Kinetiq Gym and the Kinetiq logo are the maker's names and marks. None of the licences above grants any right to use them. Only the maker sells kits, parts or machines under the Kinetiq name or logo.

If you build, modify or sell hardware based on this design:

  • give it your own name and logo, not Kinetiq or anything confusingly similar;
