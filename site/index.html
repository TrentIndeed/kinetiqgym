What Kinetiq does

A programmable digital-resistance platform, built as open hardware for people who want to train with it, build it, modify it, and program it.

  1. 01
    Programmable resistanceA motor and a cable drum stand in for the weight stack, so the load is set in software.
  2. 02
    Adjustable cable position and directionIt mounts on a rack upright at the height you want, and the cable exit swivels to follow the pull.
  3. 03
    Software-defined training modesEccentric, chain-style and band-style resistance are designed as modes, not attachments.
  4. 04
    Customizable hardwareOne bolted aluminum frame, five motor and power builds, two battery sizes, and documented interfaces for your own.
  5. 05
    Local, hackable architectureDesigned to run on its own controller and a Raspberry Pi, with no account or cloud service in the loop.

Why open

Most digital resistance systems are closed appliances. Kinetiq is being built so builders and researchers can inspect the mechanics, modify the hardware, write their own training modes, and build on the platform.

The files will be published once the physical machine has been built, tested and documented, so what you download is something that has actually worked. Until then, the build preview shows the current engineering in detail.
