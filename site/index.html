Make it yours

Change the interfaces.
Keep the system in view.

Start from a copy of the reference assembly. Adapt the mechanical envelope and mounting features together with the electrical configuration.

Measure the pack, then the bay.

  1. Measure the real pack’s length, width and height, including leads and connectors.
  2. Open the battery saddles and cover in FreeCAD. Edit the relevant feature dimensions, or create a constrained sketch around the new envelope.
  3. Keep space for padding, lead bends, the BMS and a straight removal path. Check both packs together in the assembly.
  4. Re-export STEP/STL and a revised BOM. Reassess the series count, BMS, charger and regenerative current limits before any powered test.

The reference uses two 5S packs in series (10S, 42 V fully charged). A larger pack is not automatically electrically compatible.

Preserve the shaft centerline.

  1. Measure shaft diameter and projection, pilot diameter, bolt pattern, body diameter and length.
  2. In V43_METAL_motor_face_4mm, edit the mounting features or add a constrained face sketch for the new pilot and bolt circle.
  3. Update the coupling’s motor-side bore; check the gussets, covers and phase-lead exit against the new motor envelope.
  4. Recompute the assembly and check full motion. Recalculate torque-to-cable-force and update motor, encoder, current and thermal settings before testing.

The drive is direct: cable force ≈ motor torque ÷ effective drum radius. A different Kv or larger motor does not by itself establish a safe resistance rating.

FreeCAD files currently contain editable feature histories. Fully sketch-parametric parts are planned. STEP files are solids; add sketches and features in your CAD tool to modify them.
